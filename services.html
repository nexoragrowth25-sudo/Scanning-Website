Portable & Agile

Onsite 3D Laser Scanning Service

Our onsite 3D scanning service provides rapid, metrology-grade data capture directly at your manufacturing plant, eliminating the high costs, downtime, and risk associated with shipping sensitive tools or large machinery.

Powered by the ultra-portable SIMSCAN GEN2 and NimbleTrack optical systems, we capture up to 5.8 million points per second with 0.020 mm accuracy. From complex automotive sheet metal and dies to deep recesses and shiny chrome surfaces, our scanners capture true geometry without messy sprays.

Technical Highlights:

• Accuracy: 0.020 mm (20 µm)
• Scan Rate: 5.8M measurements/s
• Weight: 570g handheld agility
• Output: High-density STL, OBJ, PLY

Large Volume Leader

Laser Tracker Inspection (Up to 80m)

When component dimensions exceed conventional CMM envelopes, our Leica Laser Tracker inspection services deliver unparalleled volumetric accuracy over working distances of up to 80 meters.

Equipped with PowerLock laser beam re-acquisition, high-speed 6DoF hand-held probing, and continuous tracking, we align large assembly fixtures, inspect aircraft wings and fuselage sections, calibrate massive machine tool beds, and verify railway chassis.

Applications:

• Aerospace fuselage & wings
• Heavy turbine casing alignment
• Automotive assembly framing jigs
• Locomotive chassis & bogie geometry

Quality Assurance

CMM & FARO Arm Inspection Services

Our Coordinate Measuring Machine (CMM) and articulated FARO Arm inspection services guarantee rigid quality assurance through automated contact probing and high-definition laser scanning.

Designed for first-article inspection (FAI), batch quality verification, and machine tool runoff verification, the FARO Quantum Arm reaches inside deep bores, undercut features, and prismatic surfaces with 0.024 mm volumetric repeatability.

Key Capabilities:

• Hard contact probe verification
• HD Laser Line Probe scanning
• 7-Axis articulated freedom
• ISO 10360-12 compliance

0.01 mm Metrology Grade

Desktop Automated 3D Scanning (AutoScan Inspec2)

Specifically engineered for small, intricate components, our desktop automated scanning service utilizes the state-of-the-art AutoScan Inspec2 with dual 5.0 MP cameras and an automated 3-axis turntable.

Achieving up to 0.010 mm (10-micron) accuracy, it automatically scans every orientation without human intervention, capturing micro-details on turbine blades, gears, medical implants, injection molded connectors, and precision jewelry.

Ideal Components:

• Precision small gears & pinions
• Micro-machined electronics molds
• Orthopedic implants & surgical tools
• Aerospace fuel nozzles & blades

Parametric CAD Recreation

Reverse Engineering & 3D CAD Modeling

Turn real-world physical parts into exact, parametric 3D CAD models. Whether you are recovering lost drawings for legacy machinery, recreating damaged tooling, or upgrading product design, our reverse engineering workflow ensures flawless design intent.

Using industry standard software like Geomagic Design X, SolidWorks, CATIA V5, and Siemens NX, our reverse engineers extract true sketches, prismatic cylinders, fillets, and organic Class-A NURBS surfaces with complete feature trees.

Deliverable Formats:

• Native: SolidWorks, CATIA, NX, Inventor
• Neutral: STEP (.stp), IGES (.igs), Parasolid (.x_t)
• Polygon: Watertight STL, OBJ, PLY for 3D Print
• 2D Prints: Manufacturing GD&T prints (.dwg, .pdf)

Traceable Quality Reports

GD&T Inspection Reports & 3D Color Deviation Maps

Raw measurement points are only useful when translated into actionable engineering insights. Our inspection reports deliver intuitive 3D color-deviation heatmaps overlaid directly onto nominal CAD models.

Generated using PolyWorks Inspector and Geomagic Control X, our reports document comprehensive Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing (GD&T) metrics, including flatness, position, perpendicularity, profile, and runout with clear pass/fail flags.

Report Inclusions:

• Nominal vs. Measured tabular data
• Visual 3D color gradient deviations
• Cross-sectional dimensional cuts
• Certified calibration certificate stamp
