Our Inception & Growth

Founded in 2020 on the Foundation of Accuracy and Reliability

Sunrise Metrology Solutions was established in 2020 with a clear objective: to make world-class 3D optical scanning, coordinate metrology, and high-precision inspection accessible to manufacturing units across India.

Whether dealing with critical aerospace turbine blades, high-speed automotive stampings, railway rolling stock, or massive power generation equipment, precision is never optional. Under the visionary direction of Amol Palande (possessing over 15 years of industry leadership), our company has successfully executed over 500+ critical metrology projects for leading Indian and multinational enterprises.

Purpose & Principles

Driven by Mission. Defined by Precision.

Our foundation rests upon technical integrity, rapid responsiveness, and uncompromising dimensional accuracy.

Our Mission

To empower Indian manufacturing industries by providing state-of-the-art 3D scanning, laser tracker alignment, and reverse engineering services directly on-site, minimizing plant downtime and ensuring zero-defect production quality.

Our Vision

To become India's foremost metrology partner of choice, recognized nationally for benchmark accuracy, technological innovation, and engineering integrity across defense, automotive, aerospace, and energy sectors.

Quality & Compliance

Certified Metrology Standards & Strict Confidentiality

We operate under rigorous quality management frameworks to ensure complete measurement traceability and absolute data protection.

ISO 9001:2015 Compliant

Every scanning, probing, and CAD inspection workflow adheres to ISO standard inspection protocols and temperature compensation procedures.

NABL Traceable Calibration

All reference artifacts, laser tracker calibration bars, and FARO arm probes undergo recurring verification traceable to national standards.

Mutual NDA & IP Protection

We execute formal Non-Disclosure Agreements prior to receiving client drawings, physical tooling, or proprietary CAD assemblies.

Transparent GD&T Reports

We deliver comprehensive 3D color deviation maps, pass/fail dimensional charts, and CAD deviation tables that are simple to interpret and act on.
