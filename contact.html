Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed answers regarding quotation procedures, onsite deployment logistics, data confidentiality, and CAD deliverables.

For manufacturing hubs in Pune (Chakan, Bhosari, Talegaon, Ranjangaon) and Bengaluru (Peenya, Whitefield, Bommasandra), we can mobilize our portable scanning team within 12 to 24 hours. For other Pan-India industrial corridors (Chennai, Delhi NCR, Ahmedabad, Hyderabad), deployment is scheduled within 24 to 48 hours depending on transit logistics. In emergency assembly line stoppages, 24/7 on-call dispatch is available.
Our portable equipment (SIMSCAN GEN2 and NimbleTrack) is self-contained. We only require a stable floor workspace, a standard single-phase 230V AC power socket, and clear physical access around the part or tool. For Leica Laser Tracker measurements on fixtures exceeding 5 meters, minimizing severe ground vibration and direct thermal draft currents helps achieve optimal micron-level repeatability.
Yes, absolutely. Intellectual Property security is a core foundation of our service. We routinely execute binding mutual NDAs with automotive OEMs, aerospace contractors, and tooling manufacturers prior to receiving proprietary 3D CAD models, 2D drawings, or physical sample components. All data is processed on air-gapped secure workstations.
We deliver fully editable parametric feature-tree models in native SolidWorks (.sldprt), CATIA V5 (.CATPart), and Siemens NX, as well as industry-standard neutral formats including STEP (.stp), IGES (.igs), and Parasolid (.x_t). For 3D printing and CFD/FEA analysis, we provide watertight polygonal STL/OBJ meshes.
Yes. Every measurement instrument in our fleet—including our Leica Laser Trackers, SIMSCAN optical heads, and FARO coordinate arms—carries valid ISO 17025 / NABL traceable annual calibration certificates. Every inspection report includes instrument serial numbers, calibration validity dates, ambient temperature compensation logs, and volumetric uncertainty statements.
Yes. Clients frequently courier or transport precision components directly to our Pune laboratory. Our temperature-controlled facility features high-resolution desktop scanning (AutoScan Inspec2 down to 0.010 mm) and precision granite surface plates. We inspect and return components with full documentation via insured logistics.
